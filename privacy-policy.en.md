## 2. The data we collect and why

This section describes every category of personal data Oglasino collects, why we collect it, and the legal basis we rely on under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection, and Montenegrin data-protection law.

### 2.1 Account data

When you create an account on Oglasino, we collect and store:

- **Email address** — required for account creation, login, and account-related notifications. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email address, we receive a forwarding address issued by Apple instead.
- **Authentication identifier (Firebase UID)** — an internal identifier issued by our authentication provider, Firebase Authentication. This identifier links your account in our database to your authentication record.
- **Display name** — a name shown on your public profile and listings. You choose it when you register with an email address; for accounts created through Google sign-in, it is taken from your Google profile, and for accounts created through Sign in with Apple, from the name Apple shares with us at your first sign-in. (If a display name is ever unavailable at registration, the part of your email address before the "@" symbol may be used as a fallback.) You can change your display name at any time in your profile settings. If you declare as a trader, other users see your business name in its place (see Section 2.2).
- **Sign-in provider** — whether you registered with email, with Google, or with Apple.
- **Apple identifier** — if you sign in with Apple, we store the permanent identifier Apple assigns to your account. We use it to prevent re-registration with the same Apple account if your account is banned (see Section 8.5).
- **Email verification status** — whether your email has been verified.
- **Password** — your password is never sent to or stored by Oglasino. It is handled exclusively by Firebase Authentication, our authentication provider, directly from your device or browser. Oglasino has no access to it.
- **Account creation and last update timestamps.**
- **Acceptance record** — the version of the Terms of Use you accepted, which is also the version of this Privacy Policy you were shown, and the date and time. We keep it on the basis of our legitimate interest in being able to show which version you accepted. It is kept while your account exists and deleted with it.

**Lawful basis:** performance of a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) — we cannot provide an account-based service without this data.

### 2.2 Profile data

In addition to account data, your profile may contain:

The time your number was verified and the channel you chose are kept for as long as that number stays verified on your account. The number you submit is not shown anywhere and is not saved on your account until you confirm the code. To limit how many codes can be requested, we keep short-lived counters per phone number, per account, and per IP address (see Section 8.1). ### 2.4 Listing data

When you create a listing, we collect and store:

- **Title and description** — written by you, in any of the platform's supported languages. We automatically translate these into the platform's other supported languages (see Section 2.8).
- **Price and currency** — Serbian dinar (RSD) and euro (EUR) are supported on the Serbian portal; euro (EUR) is supported on the Montenegrin portal.

- **Counters** — view count and favorite count for each listing. The stored counters are anonymous totals. To count each view only once, we keep a short-lived technical record combining the listing with your device identifier or IP address for up to 12 hours, after which it is automatically deleted; we do not build a history of which listings you have viewed. Separately, if you have consented to analytics, your own browsing activity is part of the analytics described in Section 2.14.

**Lawful basis:** performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) — listings are core platform functionality.

### 2.5 Messages and chat attachments

The platform includes a private messaging feature between users. We store:

- **Messages** — sender, recipient, message content, timestamp, read status. Messages are stored on Google Firestore (see Section 4).
- **Image attachments** — you may attach images to messages. These are stored on Cloudflare R2 and are accessible only through signed, time-limited links.

- **Notifications** — we notify you about relevant activity. Stored in-app notifications are deleted 90 days after they were created. What a notification contains depends on the event:
  - **Someone favorites your listing** — we store an in-app notification on Firestore and send a push notification. It names the listing but does **not** identify the user who favorited it.
  - **Someone follows you** — we store an in-app notification and send a push notification that includes the follower's display name and a link to their profile.
  - **An administrator acts on your account or content** — we store an in-app notification and send a push notification describing the action; it contains no other user's personal data.
  - **Your phone number is removed because another account verified it** — we store an in-app notification and send a push notification saying that the number was removed.
  - **An order event** — when an order is sent to you, or an order of yours is accepted, declined, cancelled, completed, expired or closed (Section 2.18), we store an in-app notification and send a push notification. It can name the other party to the order; it contains no item names and no delivery details. The notification stays in your notification list as it was written for 90 days and is then deleted; within that time it is not changed if the other party deletes their account.
  - **You receive a chat message** — we send a **push notification only**; no in-app notification record is stored. So that you can read the message from your device's notification screen, as is standard for messaging, this notification shows the sender's display name and the message text.

**Access to messages.** The platform's administrators may access message contents when investigating reports of misconduct or violations of our Terms of Use — for example, a harassment report about another user. This access happens through our administrative tools, not through any general visibility: outside of moderation work, your messages are visible only to you and the other participant in the conversation. For what happens to messages when an account is deleted, see Section 8.3.

**Lawful basis:** performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) for storing and delivering messages; legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) for administrator access to messages for moderation and safety purposes.

### 2.6 Reviews

The platform allows a user to leave a review about a seller, for one of that seller's listings. We store, for each review:

- **Reviewer** — the user who wrote the review.
- **Target user** — the user the review is about.
- **Product reference** — the listing the review is about.
- **Rating** — a number from 1 to 5.
- **Comment** — free text. We automatically translate review comments into the platform's other supported languages (see Section 2.8).
- **Images** — optional photos attached to the review, stored on Cloudflare R2.
- **Verification status** — which of two checks, if any, the review passed when it was written. "Verified order": the reviewer completed an order for that listing through Oglasino (the seller accepted it, and the buyer confirmed receipt or did not cancel within 14 days). "Verified contact": the reviewer had a real chat with the seller on Oglasino, with replies from both sides, in the 14 days before writing the review. We store which check passed, and the review is shown with that mark. We do not check payment, delivery, or the item.
- **Approval status** — whether the review has been approved by a platform administrator for public display. All reviews require administrator approval before they become visible to other users.

**Lawful basis:** performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) — reviews are part of the platform's trust system.

### 2.8 Automatic translation of your content

The platform supports four languages: Serbian, English, Russian, and Montenegrin. When you write a listing title, listing description, profile bio, or review comment in one language, we send your text to **OpenAI** (an external service based in the United States) for automatic translation into the platform's other supported languages. The translated versions are stored alongside your original text so that users on the platform can see your content in their preferred language.

These requests contain only the text to be translated, wrapped in our translation instructions — they do not include your name, email address, account identifier, or any other metadata about you, and we ask OpenAI not to store the request content. Chat messages are never sent to OpenAI.

**Category matching for imported products.** If you connect an external web shop to Oglasino (see Section 2.17), the names and descriptions of your imported products are sent to OpenAI for translation exactly as described above. In addition, to help map your shop's categories to Oglasino's categories, we send product names and descriptions together with the category names from your shop to OpenAI, which proposes matching Oglasino categories for you or our administrators to confirm. These requests follow the same conditions described above: they contain no data about you beyond that product text, and we ask OpenAI not to store the request content.

OpenAI's API terms commit that data sent through their API is not used to train their models. Oglasino itself does not train any AI model on your content (see Section 6 for our broader commitments on data use).

This processing involves transferring your text outside the European Economic Area (EEA) to the United States. See Section 5 on international transfers.

**Lawful basis:** performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) — multilingual content is a core feature of the platform.

### 2.18 Cart and orders

On listings that offer it, a buyer can put items in a cart and send an order to the seller. Oglasino passes the order between the buyer and the seller and takes no payment (see Section 1). This section describes the data involved.

**Your cart.** We store which listings are in your cart, the price each had when you added it, and when you added it. Only you can see your cart; the seller is not told that a listing is in someone's cart. An item leaves the cart when you remove it, when you send the order, or when the listing is deleted.

**What you give when you send an order.** For every order: the recipient's name and a contact phone number, and, for each seller, whether you want courier delivery or pickup and an optional note to that seller. If any seller is to deliver by courier, also the street, city, and postal code. The phone number you give here is a contact for that order only: it is not added to your profile and is not used for verification. If the recipient is another person, you give us their details on their behalf; give them only if you are entitled to.

**Saved delivery details.** The delivery form has a box "Save these details for next time". We save nothing unless you tick it yourself. If it is ticked when you send, we save the recipient's name, phone, street, city, and postal code to your account and fill the form in for you next time. Saved details are deleted automatically 30 days after you last saved them, and with your account; to have them removed sooner, write to privacy@oglasino.com.

**What the seller sees, and when.** Before accepting, the seller sees the items, your displayed name, the delivery city, courier or pickup, and your note — not the recipient's name, phone, street, or postal code. Only after accepting does the seller see the recipient's name and phone and, for courier delivery, the street and postal code. For pickup, the street and postal code are not stored on the order at all. The seller receives these details to carry out your order and may pass them to the delivery service; the Terms of Use (Section 6.6) forbid any other use. Once the seller has them, the seller answers for how they are used: a seller who is a trader is a controller of those details in their own right, and a seller who is a private person may use them only for your order. Oglasino cannot control what a seller does with the details after receiving them; if you believe a seller has misused them, report it to us.

**What the buyer sees.** The buyer sees the seller's displayed name and city, whether the seller is a trader or a private person, or that the seller has not yet declared, and, if the seller is a trader, the business name, registration number, and seat address shown on that seller's listings (Section 2.2). The tax number is not stored on an order.

**What an order keeps.** Each order keeps a copy, taken when it was sent, of: each item's name, price, currency, and cover photo reference; the buyer's displayed name; the seller's displayed name and city; and a trader's business data named above. It also keeps the order's state and dates, any message the seller or the buyer wrote when accepting, declining, or cancelling, and — if a problem was reported — the report (Section 2.9). Both parties see those messages. Each party also sees whether the other is verified on Oglasino (the verification level in Section 2.2).

**The setting for accepting orders.** Your account stores whether you accept orders through the cart. It is on by default, and you can change it at any time in your settings.

**Emails and notifications.** We send order notifications (Section 2.5) and three order emails: to the seller when an order arrives (the buyer's displayed name, the date, the items and prices); to the buyer when it is accepted (the seller's displayed name, the date, the items and prices, and the seller's message, if any); and to the other party when an accepted order is closed because an account was closed (the date, the items and prices). No order email contains an address, a phone number, or the buyer's note. These emails are essential service messages and are sent regardless of your email settings (Section 2.11). An email stays in the recipient's mailbox; we cannot erase it from there.

**Automatic steps.** An order that the seller does not answer within 48 hours expires; the seller is reminded once, after 24 hours. An accepted order that is not cancelled is marked as received 14 days after acceptance, if the buyer has not confirmed receipt earlier. If a seller lets three orders in a row expire, their setting for accepting orders is turned off automatically and they are told; they can turn it on again at any time (see Section 9). For this we keep, on the seller's account, a count of orders that expired in a row.

**Access by administrators.** Administrators can see an order in full, including the delivery details, the messages, and a report, when handling a reported problem or enforcing the Terms of Use.

For how long order data is kept, see Section 8.8.

- **Application logs**, which include IP addresses and account identifiers (see Section 2.12), are retained for **90 days**, after which they are purged.

### 8.3 When you delete your account

Account deletion on Oglasino is a two-stage process designed to give you a meaningful change-of-mind window and, equally important, to give other users a fair last chance to report you if they believe you have acted in bad faith on the platform.

**Stage 1 — Soft deletion (7 days).** When you request account deletion from your settings, your account immediately enters a "scheduled for deletion" state:

- **Your profile remains publicly visible** and is marked with a "Scheduled for deletion" status badge so that other users can see you have requested deletion. We deliberately keep your profile visible during this window, rather than hiding it, so that users who have interacted with you still have the opportunity to file a report if they have a legitimate concern about your conduct.
- **Your phone number is hidden** during this window, so that you are not contacted while your deletion is pending.
- **Your listings are hidden** from public view.
- **Reviews about you remain visible** so that the context for any potential reports is preserved.
- **You cannot send or receive messages** during the grace period. Existing conversations remain accessible to the other participants for reporting purposes, but no new messages can be sent to or from your account.
- **Your open orders are cancelled.** Orders that are waiting for an answer or are accepted are cancelled at once, whether you are the buyer or the seller, and the other party is told. They are not restored if you restore your account.
- **Reports against you remain open.** Any user who interacted with you can still file a report during the grace period.
- You are signed out.

During these 7 days, you can restore your account at any time simply by signing back in. We do this so that you do not lose your data because of an accidental deletion, a moment of frustration, or a change of mind. If you sign in during the grace period, your profile, listings, phone-number visibility, and messaging are all restored.

**Stage 2 — Hard deletion (after 7 days).** On the 8th day, your account is permanently deleted, subject to the postponement exceptions described in Section 8.4. Hard deletion means:

- Your account record, listings, listing images, favorites, follow relationships, stored push tokens, and in-app notifications are permanently erased from our databases and storage.
- Your seller status, business data, and the full seller declaration history are permanently erased together with your account record.
- Your profile picture is deleted from our storage.
- Your authentication record at Firebase is deleted.
- **Chat messages are anonymized rather than erased.** Your conversations are part of the other participant's message history too, so the message content remains available to them — but your identity is removed: your account's references in those conversations are replaced with an anonymous marker, shown to other users as "Deleted User." The same applies to images you attached to messages.
- **Reviews you have left about other users:** approved reviews are kept, with your name replaced by "Deleted User" — they reflect the experience of the user you reviewed, and erasing them would erase part of their record. We disclose this here so that, when you leave a review, you know an approved review survives your account deletion (anonymized). Reviews of yours that were still awaiting approval, or were declined, are deleted. Reviews about you are deleted.
- **Your cart, saved delivery details, and orders:** your cart and your saved delivery details are deleted. What happens to your orders is described in Section 8.8.
- **Residual copies.** Copies of deleted content can persist for a limited time in technical systems that refresh on their own schedule — for example, edge caches of images and entries in our search index — until those systems expire or re-synchronize.

After hard deletion, the only data we retain are the limited audit records described in Section 8.5, the order data described in Section 8.8, and, if your account was banned, the anonymized report records described in Section 8.6.

### 8.4 When deletion may be postponed

In limited circumstances, we may delay hard deletion of your account beyond the standard 7-day grace period. This applies only in two cases:

- **Legal investigations.** When a law-enforcement authority (such as the police or a court) asks us to preserve your data in connection with an ongoing investigation, we are legally required to comply and we will postpone deletion for as long as the legal request requires.
- **Internal abuse investigations.** When we have reason to believe your account has been used for serious abuse of the platform — for example, fraud against multiple users — and we are actively investigating, we may postpone deletion to preserve evidence relevant to the investigation.

In both cases, if your deletion is postponed, we will tell you that the deletion has been delayed and, to the extent we are legally permitted to do so, why. You will continue to be signed out and unable to use the account during the postponement; the only effect is that hard deletion is paused. Once the legal or internal investigation concludes, hard deletion proceeds.

**Lawful basis for postponement:** legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) for law-enforcement preservation requests, and legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) for internal abuse investigations.

### 8.5 Audit records retained after deletion or ban

- **A general deletion audit record** — a hashed version of your user identifier and email (using SHA-256, a one-way hash that cannot be reversed to recover your original email), along with the times of the deletion steps, what started the deletion (for example your own request or an administrator), and whether the account was banned. This is kept for **30 days** after deletion, then automatically purged.
- **Banned-user audit record** — if your account is banned, we record, at the moment the ban is issued, a hashed version of your email (or, if your account was created through Sign in with Apple without an email address, a hashed version of your Apple identifier), the reason for the ban as the administrator wrote it, and whether you were told (and, if not, why not). This record is used to prevent banned users from re-registering with the same email address or Apple account, and is retained for **12 months** from the ban, then automatically purged. It remains in place if the banned account is later deleted, and it is deleted at once if the ban is lifted.
- **Administrator-action record** — when an administrator deletes an account or lifts a ban, we record a hashed version of the user identifier and email, the action, the reason as the administrator wrote it, whether the user was told (and, if not, why not), and the time. It is kept for **12 months**, then automatically purged.
- **Listing-action record** — when an administrator removes a listing, deletes it permanently, or changes its category, we record the listing's number, a hashed version of the owner's user identifier, the reason as the administrator wrote it, whether the owner was told (and, if not, why not), the time, and, for a change of category, the old and the new category and price. It is kept for **12 months**, then automatically purged.

The banned-user record, the administrator-action record and the listing-action record also note which administrator acted. Apart from the reasons and the listing details named above, these records contain no readable personal data — only one-way hashes. A reason is a short note by the administrator on what was done and why; administrators are told not to name any person in it. A hash cannot be reversed to reveal your email address, although it can be compared against a known email address (that comparison is exactly how the re-registration check works). After the retention period expires, the whole record is deleted, the hash included. The purge runs once a week, so a record can stay up to seven days longer than its period.

### 8.6 Reports filed by or about users

While the accounts involved are active, reports are retained so that administrators can detect patterns of repeat abuse; we currently keep them without a fixed time limit, and we plan to introduce automatic purging of resolved reports after a set period. What happens to reports when an account involved in them is permanently deleted depends on whether that account was banned:

- If the deleted account was **not banned**, reports filed by it and about it are deleted together with the account.
- If the deleted account was **banned**, the reports are kept — with all account references removed, linked only to the anonymized ban record from Section 8.5 — and are deleted together with that record, at the latest 12 months after the ban.

### 8.7 If your account is banned

If we permanently ban your account (see the Terms of Use, Section 10.2), the ban takes effect immediately: your public profile and phone number stop being shown, your listings are hidden, your open orders are cancelled, and your sign-in is disabled. The ban does not by itself trigger the deletion process — we retain the account's data for as long as it is needed to document the violation, handle any appeal, protect other users, and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. You can request erasure of a banned account at any time (see Section 9), subject to the postponement cases in Section 8.4. A banned-user audit record (Section 8.5) is created when the ban is issued and is retained for 12 months.

**Lawful basis:** legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) — platform safety and the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

### 8.8 Orders

- **Your cart** is kept until you empty it or send the order, and is deleted with your account.
- **Saved delivery details** are deleted automatically 30 days after you last saved them, and with your account (Section 2.18).
- **Delivery details and texts on an order.** 90 days after an order closes — is completed, declined, cancelled, or expired — we erase from it the recipient's name, phone, street, city, and postal code, the buyer's note, the accept, decline, and cancel messages, and the text of any report. A problem report about an order is kept for 30 days from the day it was filed, even where the 90-day deletion falls earlier.
- **The rest of the order** — the items and prices, the displayed names, a trader's business data, the state, and the dates — stays visible to the buyer and the seller in their order lists for as long as their accounts exist.
- **If the buyer's account is deleted**, the buyer's open orders are cancelled, and on all of the buyer's orders the buyer's name, the delivery details, the buyer's note, and any cancel message or report the buyer wrote are erased at once. The seller keeps the order, with the buyer shown as "Deleted User".
- **If the seller's account is deleted**, the seller's open orders are cancelled, and any accept, decline, or cancel message or report the seller wrote is erased at once. On orders the seller never accepted, the seller's name and business data are erased and the buyer sees "Deleted User". On orders the seller accepted at any time, the seller's displayed name, city, seller status, and business data, as they were when the order was sent, stay visible to the buyer for three years after the order was accepted, so that the buyer knows whom they ordered from and can bring a claim; after that the buyer sees "Deleted User".
- **Administrator-only record.** When a seller with at least one accepted order is deleted, we keep, for each such order, a record visible only to administrators: the seller's displayed name (the business name for a trader), email address, and the phone number from the seller's profile, if there was one. It exists so that a buyer's claim or a request from an authority can still be answered after the seller's account is gone, and it is deleted after 12 months. We disclose it only on a legally binding request from a court or another authority.
- **When both accounts are deleted**, the order itself is deleted — shortly afterwards if it was never accepted, otherwise together with the administrator-only record.
- **Notifications and emails.** None of the above changes an order notification already delivered to the other party or an order email already sent (Sections 2.5 and 2.18). A delivered notification stays in the notification list for 90 days and is then deleted.

## 9. Your rights

Under GDPR and Serbian and Montenegrin data-protection law, you have the following rights about your personal data. We support each of them.

**Right of access.** You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. Email privacy@oglasino.com with your request. We will respond within one month, as required by GDPR.

**Right to rectification.** You can correct most of your personal data directly in your profile settings. For data you cannot self-edit (such as moderation-related information), email privacy@oglasino.com and we will correct any inaccuracies.

**Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten").** You can delete your account at any time from your account settings; see Section 8.3 for what this involves. If you are unable to use the self-service deletion flow — for example, because your account has been banned — contact support@oglasino.com to request deletion and we will respond within one month, as required by GDPR. For all other privacy-related deletion enquiries, email privacy@oglasino.com.

**Right to restriction of processing.** You can ask us to limit how we process your personal data — for example, while you contest a moderation decision. Email privacy@oglasino.com with your request and the reason.

**Right to data portability.** You can request a copy of the personal data you have provided to us, in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (such as JSON). This right applies to data you provided directly to us (your profile, listings, messages, reviews, orders); it does not extend to data we derived about you (such as your rating or audit records). Email privacy@oglasino.com to request a portable export.

**Right to object.** You can object to our processing of your data when we rely on legitimate interest. You can object to receiving promotional emails by turning off the corresponding toggle in your settings, and we will always honor that. You can object to the use of your public content in Oglasino's promotion at any time, without giving a reason, by writing to support@oglasino.com or privacy@oglasino.com, and we will always honor that too (Section 2.19). For other objections, email privacy@oglasino.com and we will assess your objection.

**Right to withdraw consent.** Where we rely on your consent (cookies and analytics, communication preferences), you can withdraw it at any time:

- Cookie and analytics preferences on the website: on the cookie-preferences page at `/owner/cookies` (linked from your account settings), or by reopening the consent banner from the "Manage cookies" control in the website footer.
- Analytics in the mobile app: through the analytics setting in the app.
- Communication preferences: through the toggles on your account settings page.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of any processing we did before withdrawal.

**Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.** If you believe we have not handled your personal data lawfully, you have the right to complain to a data-protection authority. In Serbia, the relevant authority is the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (Poverenik za informacije od javnog značaja i zaštitu podataka o ličnosti) — https://www.poverenik.rs/. In Montenegro, the relevant authority is the Agency for Personal Data Protection and Free Access to Information (Agencija za zaštitu ličnih podataka i slobodan pristup informacijama) — https://www.azlp.me/. If you are in the EU, you can also complain to the data-protection authority in your country of residence.

**How we respond to requests.** We aim to respond to all data-subject requests within one month of receiving them. In limited cases involving particularly complex requests, GDPR allows us to extend this by up to two further months, in which case we will tell you within the first month. We keep the emails you send to privacy@oglasino.com or support@oglasino.com, and our replies, for three years, and then delete them.

**Automated decision-making.** Oglasino does not make significant decisions about you using purely automated processes, with two narrow exceptions described below. Content validation runs automatically when you create or edit a listing (for example, checking for prohibited content), but it can only reject the submission with an error message — enforcement decisions such as removing a listing, banning an account, or resolving a report are made by a human administrator. When you save a listing, an automatic check for prohibited words can stop it from being saved. You are told at once, you can change the text and try again, and you can write to support if you think the check is wrong. The first exception: when an account's sign-in record no longer exists at our authentication provider (an "orphaned" account) and that account has multiple unresolved reports against it, our cleanup process removes the orphaned account and automatically records the re-registration block described in Section 8.5, without an individual human decision. The second exception: if you have listings or a connected web shop and do not declare your seller status within 30 days of the first reminder (Section 2.4), an automated daily job deactivates your listings and pauses your shop's synchronization, without an individual human decision; you can undo this yourself at any time by declaring your status. Some steps in the cart run automatically but are not decisions of that kind: an order that is not answered expires, an accepted order is marked as received after a fixed period, and if you let three orders in a row expire, your setting for accepting orders is turned off. None of them affects your rights, and you can turn the setting on again yourself at any time (Section 2.18). If we introduce further automated decision-making in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and loss. These include:

- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS for all connections between your browser and our services).
- Encryption of data at rest by our infrastructure providers (Firebase, Firestore, Cloudflare R2, Postgres on DigitalOcean).
- Authentication and authorization controls limiting access to user data, including signed, time-limited access to private images.
- Logging of administrative access and administrative actions.
- Hashing of stored email addresses and identifiers in our deletion and ban audit records.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a personal data breach that creates a risk to your rights, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority and, where required by GDPR, notify you directly.
